## About

I am having fun at [Fondazione Bruno Kessler (FBK)](https://www.fbk.eu/it/) working on efficient multi-modal learning.  I am part of Deep Visual Learning (DVL) unit, advised by Prof. [Elisa Ricci](https://eliricci.eu/) and Dr. [Yiming Wang](https://www.yimingwang.me/).

Before joining FBK I obtained the PhD in Computer Vision at University of Genova and Italian Institute of Technology (IIT) under the supervision of Dr. [Alessio Del Bue](https://www.iit.it/people/alessio-delbue) and Prof. [Stuart James](https://stuart-james.com/). During the PhD, I visited the University of Amsterdam to work with Prof. [Sara Magliacane](https://saramagliacane.github.io/). I completed both the BS in Information Engineering and the MS in Computer Engineering at University of Padova.

My research interests are on **representation learning**, **multimodal large language models** and making them more **human-centric**.
